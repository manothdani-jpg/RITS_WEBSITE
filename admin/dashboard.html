Square Payments

How members pay online for dues, events, and cultural registrations. Only super admins can change this.

Also used for cultural registrations and card payments at the front desk.

Members see the exact amount they owe, then are sent to the link. A link can't tell this site who paid, so payments stay pending until you confirm them with Mark paid under Reports → Recent Payments (that registers the person, updates dues and the expiry date, and emails them). Make sure each link lets the payer type in an amount.

The site creates an exact-amount Square checkout for every payment, and Square confirms it automatically — no manual step. This needs SQUARE_ACCESS_TOKEN and SQUARE_LOCATION_ID set on the server.
