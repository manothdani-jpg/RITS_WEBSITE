What we do

Four pillars of RITS

Language & Literature

Tamil classes, spelling bees, and literary events that keep our mother tongue thriving in the next generation.

Arts & Culture

Classical and folk dance, music, and festival celebrations like Pongal and Tamil New Year.

Community & Fellowship

Regular gatherings that connect Tamil families across Rhode Island and build lasting friendships.

Youth & Education

Scholarships, mentorship, and a youth wing that gives the next generation ownership of RITS's future.
